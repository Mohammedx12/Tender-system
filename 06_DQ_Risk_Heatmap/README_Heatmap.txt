DQ Risk Heatmap – How to Use
1) Fill PASS/FAIL/NA in the 'Precheck' sheet.
2) Assign an owner + add evidence notes for every FAIL.
3) Review 'Heatmap' for area risk levels and the OVERALL stop/go signal.
Execution rule:
- OVERALL RED: stop submission until fixes are done.
- OVERALL AMBER: fix within 48–72 hours and re-check.
- OVERALL GREEN: proceed; maintain evidence links.
